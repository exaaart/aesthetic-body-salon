Audience: visitors choosing a premium salon; action: book a procedure.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet editorial salon page where light serif type and one large photograph carry the luxury; refuses the card-grid beauty template with pink gradients and icon tiles.
OWN-WORLD: Plum-black ground #120D17 with #1B1421 bands, hairline #34263D rules, lilac #B879E8 used only on actions, soft lilac #D7B6F2 for states. Cormorant Garamond 300 display, Manrope 400/500 body. Radius 2px, no shadows, no glass.
STORY: The visitor sees the name and «Ухоженная кожа без спешки», understands the four directions, feels the interior and reviews, books in a modal that tells the truth about its state.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left five columns: two-line h1 at up to 6rem with the second line indented; subtitle; one primary button. Right seven columns: full-height editorial portrait bleeding to the viewport edge, dissolved into the ground by a left-edge mask. Transparent header above.
FORM: pinned by the brief, seed: none (pinned) — «Стиль: Minimal Luxury / Modern Editorial», palette table and section structure in sections 1–4 of the brief.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
